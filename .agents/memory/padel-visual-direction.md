---
name: Padel visual direction
description: The Padel experience uses Oliva's warm olive, cream, and gold visual language while preserving the existing court/product imagery and descriptions.
---

Padel should feel like a playful extension of Oliva's café identity rather than a separate cyan sports microsite. Keep the existing CMS-driven items, prices, descriptions, and images, but use warm olive/gold surfaces, rounded interactive cards, gentle motion, and a clean mobile stack.

**Why:** The Padel page needs to belong to the same brand DNA as the café while still feeling energetic and sport-oriented.

**How to apply:** Preserve the content contract and route behavior when iterating; change presentation through scoped `.padel-*` styles and motion rather than removing or replacing the current media/content.
