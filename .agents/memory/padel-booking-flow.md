name: Padel offer booking
description: Court and coaching offers open WhatsApp with a fixed message; Grip and Ball Set are display-only accessories.
---

Padel court and coaching cards remain selectable and reveal the booking action with the approved fixed WhatsApp message. Grip and Ball Set are display-only, cannot be clicked or focused as buttons, and show “Ask the coach 😄” instead of a pick action.

**Why:** Court reservations and coaching use WhatsApp, while accessory cards should guide visitors to ask staff instead of behaving like booking controls.

**How to apply:** Normalize CMS accessory names to exactly “Grip” and “Ball Set,” use the approved Grip image, and keep those cards outside the selection/booking interaction. Keep the approved Padel WhatsApp message and number only for court/coaching cards.
